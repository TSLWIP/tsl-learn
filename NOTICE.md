# Notice

TSL Learn is an unofficial study tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by National Chung Cheng University.

## Sign videos and glosses

The sign clips and their Chinese and English glosses come from the **Taiwan Sign Language Online Dictionary**
(台灣手語線上辭典), National Chung Cheng University, <https://twtsl.ccu.edu.tw/>. The clips carry the
"TSL@CCU" mark. The dictionary and its construction are described in Tsay (2019), *Taiwan Sign Language Online
Dictionary Construction and Expansion*.

**Reuse terms:** the dictionary site does not state any, and none were found elsewhere. This repository does not
include the clips. Do not re-host them publicly without written permission from the dictionary's maintainers.
The app is designed to fetch clips slowly from a configurable source (`public/content-source.json`) and cache them
on the learner's own device.
