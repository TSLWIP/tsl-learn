# Phone check

Everything so far was tested in desktop Chrome and Chromium. These are the things only a real phone can prove.
Tick them off on the iPhone you will actually use; each takes a minute or two. If anything fails, copy
**Settings → Error log → Copy** and send it along with what you did.

## First launch

- [ ] Open the site in **Safari**. The three onboarding screens appear and the text is comfortable to read.
- [ ] **Share → Add to Home Screen.** The icon is the white 手 on terracotta and the name is "TSL Learn".
- [ ] Open it from the home-screen icon. It opens full screen (no Safari bars) and shows Learn.
- [ ] **Settings → Offline packs → Test the clip source** says the clip source works.

## Clips and playback

- [ ] **Learn new** → the clip starts by itself, silently, **inside the page** (iOS must not jump to its full-screen player).
- [ ] Tapping the clip pauses it; tapping again resumes. Speed buttons 0.5×, 0.75× and 1× change the speed.
- [ ] In **Low Power Mode** a big ▶ button appears instead of autoplay and works.
- [ ] A **Meaning → sign** question shows four clips at once, and they all play.
- [ ] Rotate the phone: nothing is cut off. Everything is reachable with one thumb, including the tab bar.

## Offline

- [ ] Watch a few signs, then switch on **Airplane Mode** and open the app from the home-screen icon. It opens, and
      clips you already watched play.
- [ ] A clip you have not watched shows a clear message with "Try again", not a blank or wrong video.
- [ ] Switch Airplane Mode off. "Try again" now works.
- [ ] **Settings → Offline packs → Download** the *Etiquette* pack. Lock the phone halfway. Reopen: **Download rest**
      finishes it, and nothing is duplicated.

## Keeping your data

- [ ] After a Learn session, **Progress** shows today's answers and a 1-day run.
- [ ] **Settings → Backup → Export progress** saves a file you can find in Files. Note what **Settings → Storage →
      Protected from clean-up** says: on iPhone it may say "Not guaranteed", which is why backups matter.
- [ ] Close the app completely and open it again: your progress is still there.
- [ ] Use it for a few days, then leave it a week or two and reopen. Progress, decks and downloaded clips should all
      still be there. (This is the failure the first version suffered from, so it is the most important check.)

## After an update

- [ ] After a new version is deployed, open the app: a banner says "A new version is ready" with a Reload button, and
      after Reload your progress is intact.

## Looks and feel

- [ ] Dark mode follows the phone, and **Settings → Appearance** can force light or dark.
- [ ] With the phone's text size increased (Settings → Display → Text Size), nothing overlaps.
- [ ] With **Reduce Motion** on, clips start paused with a ▶ button and there are no animations.
